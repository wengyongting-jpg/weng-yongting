SalesPilot — Agentic WhatsApp Sales Opportunity Assistant

Role: Product Concept & Early Build  ·  Status: Hackathon demo build (repo + video + 2 deployed surfaces public)  ·  Version 1.0

01 · PROBLEM

Valuable signals stay buried in fragmented WhatsApp conversations

Sales reps receive high enquiry volume through WhatsApp with limited capacity. Routine questions (availability, pricing, delivery, hours) consume the time that should go to the signals that matter: purchase intent, hesitation, competitor comparison, expansion opportunity, risk.

  • Purchase intent signals scattered across long chat histories.
  • Hesitation and competitor comparison invisible without manual review.
  • Uniform treatment of every message wastes scarce sales capacity.
  • No escalation path for high-value or negotiation-stage situations.

02 · PRODUCT POSITIONING

Not a reply-generation chatbot — an opportunity-intelligence agent.

After every customer message, SalesPilot recalls conversation memory, detects the customer's current state, extracts sales signals, updates the Opportunity Profile, recomputes the 0–100 Opportunity Value Score, and picks the next best action — an automated AI response or a deliberate handoff to a human seller — while keeping the dashboard in sync.

North Star: rep time spent on high-value conversations as a share of total rep time.

03 · KEY PRODUCT DECISIONS

DECISION 01

Full automation, or human-in-the-loop for high-value conversations?

Compared: A · Fully automated replies for all enquiries. B · AI handles routine; humans take high-value / uncertain / negotiation cases.

Chosen: B — trust and deal size outweigh speed. The system recommends, the human decides, for anything high-stakes.

Validate via: pilot with 1–2 sales reps; log acceptance / override rate of AI next-best-actions.

DECISION 02

Continuous 0–100 score, or 3-tier hot / warm / cold buckets?

Chosen: continuous score — reps' real question is "who do I call first", which needs ranking, not labels.

Validate via: in the rep pilot, measure whether reps work the inbox top-down in score order or re-sort it.

DECISION 03

Stateful opportunity profile, or stateless per-message replies?

Chosen: stateful — purchase intent and hesitation only make sense across a conversation, not in a single message.

Validate via: score stability across a conversation — no wild swings on courtesy messages.

05 · VALIDATION STATUS & PLAN

Current: hackathon demonstration build — scripted WhatsApp scenarios + judge walkthroughs. Public artefacts: GitHub repository, demo video, admin dashboard, customer chat. No production adoption or business metrics are claimed.

Known failure modes found in testing: auto-reply fired on a negotiation-stage message (fixed with a hard handoff rule); score inflated by polite small-talk (fixed by dampening courtesy signals).

Next: rep pilot on real inbound conversations → feed rep overrides back into signal weights → controlled conversion comparison before claiming any uplift.
