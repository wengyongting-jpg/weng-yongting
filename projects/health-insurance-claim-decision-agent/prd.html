01 · WHY EVALUATION IS THE PRODUCT HERE

Claim decisions are safety-sensitive — testing is part of safety design

An AI claim-decision system must handle incomplete, adversarial, ambiguous, and escalation-triggering inputs without producing unsafe or unsupported decisions. The evaluation suite is not an afterthought — it is the mechanism that makes the system safe to operate.

  • Adversarial inputs must not produce unsupported claim decisions.
  • Ambiguous cases need safe escalation, not confident guessing.
  • Behaviour changes across versions must be comparable and reproducible.
  • Unsafe behaviour should surface in failure analysis, not production.

02 · EVALUATION PIPELINE

A repeatable pipeline: scripted case → guardrail gate → expected-outcome comparison

STAGEWHAT HAPPENS
1 · Scripted CaseA fixed claim scenario (with known expected outcome) drives the agent.
2 · Agent DecisionThe agent processes the case end-to-end and produces a decision.
3 · Guardrail CheckSafety guardrails gate the behaviour before it is accepted.
4 · Outcome ComparisonResult is compared against the expected outcome and recorded.
5 · BranchUnsafe or uncertain → failure analysis or safe escalation.

04 · REPRODUCIBILITY & VERSION COMPARISON

  • Session-based history — every run is recorded with its configuration, so results are comparable across versions.
  • Transactional result writing — results are written atomically; partial runs can't corrupt the record.
  • Reproducible failure testing — any failure case can be re-run deterministically to confirm a fix.
  • Live-model evaluation — scripted cases can also run against the live LLM to catch drift between deterministic and live behaviour.
